\paragraph{Decision models.} \jev{} was called through its vendor API; every one of the \fsJevFsCalls{} 24-shot
and \fsJevZsCalls{} zero-shot scored calls was served by the same model version, which we record per call. We never
trained or tuned anything on its outputs. \clef{} and \flash{} were scored with their release code. \pplx{} was
scored with its release code on the reproduction's 24-shot bodies; its other cells used a serving engine that first
passed our parity gate on the full Banking77 test set (argmax agreement \fsPplxGateAgree\%, maximum absolute
probability difference \fsPplxGateMaxDp). Table~\ref{tab:systems} summarises the systems.

\paragraph{\orb, the open 24-shot baseline.} For each test utterance, BM25 \citep{robertson-zaragoza-2009-bm25}
retrieves 24 labelled examples from a pool of \fsPoolN{} training utterances (the training split minus a held-out
selection split of \fsSelN{} utterances), with unigram tokens and no per-class cap. A Qwen3-4B reader
\citep{yang-etal-2025-qwen3} with a LoRA adapter \citep{hu-etal-2021-lora} scores all 77 options in one forward pass
\citep{robinson-wingate-2023-mcp}; the adapter was trained on other intent and text-classification tasks with
Banking77 excluded by exact and near-duplicate filtering. A bge-large \citep{xiao-etal-2024-cpack} nearest-neighbour
vote over the same 24 examples \citep{xu-etal-2023-knn-prompting} is combined with the reader by a weighted mean of
temperature-scaled probabilities. Members, combination rule, option text (descriptions) and the number of option
orders were chosen once, out of fold on the selection split, before the single test run
(Appendix~\ref{app:protocol}). We report calibration on the stored combined probabilities, which is conservative:
the pre-registered final temperature lowers ECE further but is not used in any comparison.

\paragraph{\gj, the zero-shot baseline.} Three encoders score the label names or descriptions: an NLI cross-encoder
(PrismNLI-0.4B; \citealp{jung-etal-2025-prismatic}) and two bi-encoders (bge-large and bge-base). Each member's
scores pass through a softmax with temperature one and the members are pooled by an equal-weight geometric mean,
with no per-task weights or temperatures. Appendix~\ref{app:jury} documents the jury in full.

\paragraph{Fixed-budget fine-tuning reference.} To place the few-shot numbers, we fine-tuned Qwen3-4B with LoRA on
the same \fsPoolN{} pool utterances for two epochs (\fsFtGpuHours{} GPU-hours) and scored the test set once. Its
validation accuracy was still rising when the budget ended, so it is a reference, not a ceiling.

\paragraph{Data.} Banking77 \citep{casanueva-etal-2020-efficient} has \fsN{} test utterances over 77 intents and
\fsFullTrain{} training utterances; all systems are scored on the full test split. The reproduction's 24-shot
request bodies use word and bigram BM25 over the full training split with at most four examples per class, and its
own label definitions \citep{smith-2026-jev-banking77}. Zero-shot results cover seven intent datasets
(\S\ref{sec:res-zeroshot}).
The thirteen code and workflow tasks, their sources, licences and sizes are described with their results
(\S\ref{sec:res-13task}, Table~\ref{tab:d13}).
Bitext customer support \citep{bitext-2023-customer-support} (CDLA-Sharing-1.0) has \btTotalN{} utterances over % numbers-ok: licence name
\btK{} intents and no official test split; we use a deterministic stratified \btTestPct\% test split (\btN{} items)
for both its zero-shot and its 24-shot results (\S\ref{sec:res-bitext}).
